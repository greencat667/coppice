# Learning log

> How the assistant's way of working is being adjusted, one small change at a time. **Active entries only**: when an entry is confirmed and graduated, or discarded, move it to `memory/archive/learning-log-archive.md` with its verdict. The weekly review does one "promote, prune or park" pass.
>
> - **Assistant adds** an entry when it notices a recurring friction or something that consistently works, after asking: *"Worth adding to the learning log?"*
> - **You add** an entry by saying *"log that"*.
> - **Stages:** `trying` (a hypothesis to test) → `confirmed` (seen enough to trust) → **graduated** into `START-HERE.md`, `soul.md` or a skill, as a proposal you approve. Or `discarded`, with the reason.
