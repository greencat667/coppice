# Handoff prompt

> Moving a line of thinking from a chat (on your phone, or another assistant) into this workspace. Attach this file to the chat and say: **"Apply this to our conversation."** Paste the result into a new session here as your opening message.

Review our conversation and produce a handoff using the format below. Keep it under half a page: concrete and specific, no general summaries.

---

## Handoff — [topic in 4–6 words]

**Context:** What we were exploring and why (2–3 sentences)

**Where we got to:** Conclusions, decisions made, things ruled out

**The live question:** The one question or task the next session should tackle

**Starting point:** The first specific action for the next session
